How to Play

🗺

The Board

9 territories connected in a network. Each has a point value (1-5). You and the AI take turns competing for control.

🏴

Claim

Place your flag on any unclaimed territory. This gives you +2 base influence there. Free — but visible to your opponent.

🃏

Fortify

Play a card from your hand face-down on ANY territory. Your opponent sees you placed a card, but not its strength (1–4).

🎭

Bluff

Drop a strength-1 card on the AI’s territory — will it waste a strong card defending? Every placement is a mind game.

⚡

Resolution

When all actions are done, every card flips. Highest total influence wins each territory. Claim (+2) plus card strengths. Ties go to the flag holder.

🔗

Networks

Control 3+ connected territories for bonus points: 3-chain = +3, 4-chain = +5, 5-chain = +8. One well-placed card can shatter a chain.
